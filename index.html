How voting works

Ticket/heart/star systems: Many apps give a limited number of free votes per day (hearts or stars) that reset at a fixed time. Some allow bonus votes through ads, missions, or purchases.

Ranking charts: Platforms like Fandom Chart or Fan N Star combine streaming, social, and voting data into one score — voting is only part of the ranking.

Account-based voting: Most official apps require an account so each person's votes are counted fairly and to prevent bots.

One-time vs daily voting: Some award shows only allow one vote per round, while apps like Idol Champ or Mubeat allow daily voting until the period ends.

FAQ
Do I need a real phone number?Some apps require a local phone number or Google/Apple sign-in — noted per voting's tutorial.
What are voting currencies?Hearts, stars, tickets, or points refer to how many votes you can submit.
Why didn't my vote count?Common causes: VPN use, an unverified account, or voting after the daily reset window closed.
Is this an official voting platform?No — this site only tracks and links to official voting pages/apps. Every card shows its verification status and source.
Why are some weekly/monthly votes missing?This tracker focuses on important official voting events — major awards and limited-time votes — not endless popularity polls.
